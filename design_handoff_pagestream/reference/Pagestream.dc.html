Terms

Demo terms · 4 sections

The texts

All eight books are public-domain texts from Project Gutenberg.

The price

Pages 1 and 2 of every book are free. Every page after that costs 5 sats, the same for every book. Each page is paid from your own BSV wallet when you open it.

Payments are final

There are no refunds in this demo. A page you paid for stays yours and reopens free with the same wallet.

Your wallet is your account

There is no sign-up, email, password, card or subscription.

Privacy

3 things stored · nothing else

We store your wallet's public identity key, the pages you bought, and your bookmark. Nothing else.
