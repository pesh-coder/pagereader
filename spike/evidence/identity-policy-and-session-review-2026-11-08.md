## Identity finding

The official [BRC-208 security considerations](https://github.com/bsv-blockchain/BRCs/blob/master/wallet/0208.md#security-considerations) recommend one active device per account. Two independently maintained local states for the same key can conflict in coin selection and miss each other's outputs. This is technical coordination guidance using SHOULD, not a blanket legal prohibition on an identity ever appearing on more than one device.

[BRC-249](https://github.com/bsv-blockchain/BRCs/blob/master/wallet/0249.md) describes account holding/handoff between devices, explicitly as a draft with a proposed number rather than an assigned standard. It is not proof of support in the installed wallets. The [Desktop README](https://github.com/bsv-blockchain/bsv-desktop#remote-storage-wab) also advertises multi-device sync for its remote-storage configuration; that does not establish safe use of two independent local stores.

No particular law, jurisdiction or vendor policy establishing the claimed blanket regulatory ban has been supplied or verified. The specific message/link has been requested. Do not advise copying wallet keys, changing wallets or bypassing device coordination to finish this demo.

**Scope decision:** C8 is waived by the owner, not passed by an implementation test. Desktop and Browser remain separate reader identities. Their bookmarks, entitlements and spending must remain separate; no automatic account linking or merging is introduced. Both devices must still work independently, with accurate spending. The original same-person/new-device/shared-bookmark story is no longer an acceptance gate for this POC.
